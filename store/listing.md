# Chrome Web Store Listing

## Name
Tab Declutter

## Short Description (132 char max)
Organize, group, and bulk-close your Chrome tabs with ease.

## Detailed Description
Tab Declutter helps you take control of your browser tabs. View all your open tabs grouped by domain, smart categories, or window, and close the ones you don't need anymore.

Features:
- Group tabs by domain, window, or smart categories (Shopping, Development, Social Media, News, and more)
- Find and close duplicate tabs with one click, keeping one copy of each URL
- Visual age indicators showing how recently each tab was accessed
- Colored dots tracking tabs opened from the same parent
- Select individual tabs or entire groups with checkboxes
- Bulk close all selected tabs at once
- View and reopen previously closed tabs from history
- Full keyboard navigation support
- Quick access via Cmd+Shift+Y (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+Y (Windows/Linux)

Your data stays local. Tab Declutter stores selections and history in your browser's local storage only. No data is collected, transmitted, or shared.
